04 / About Carter

The person
behind the music.

I'm Carter Cruz, a percussionist and composer currently studying Percussion Performance at The Hartt School.

Percussion has shaped the way I listen to music. Rhythm, tone, articulation, dynamics, and texture all influence the way a piece communicates. Those same details inspire my interest in composition and the possibilities of combining sound with storytelling.

One of my lifelong goals is to compose music for film. I'm working toward that goal through performance, studying music, writing, and exploring different approaches to composition.

My musical interests include orchestral music, ambient music, and other styles depending on the needs of a piece. I enjoy being flexible and exploring different creative directions rather than limiting myself to one sound.

Music can tell a story before a single word is spoken.

05 / Work with me

Let's create
something original.

I am open to composition commissions and enjoy working with people who have a musical idea, a story to tell, or a particular atmosphere they want to create.

Whether you need an orchestral piece, ambient music, percussion-focused writing, or something in a different style, I'm flexible and interested in adapting to the needs of the project.

If you have an idea, feel free to reach out with the intended use, instrumentation, approximate length, timeline, and any musical references you have.
