03 / Behind the sound

The person
behind the music.

I'm Carter Cruz, a percussionist whose interests extend beyond the stage and into the world of recording, audio, and musical storytelling.

My background in percussion has shaped how I listen: the smallest details in rhythm, articulation, texture, and dynamics can change the feeling of an entire piece. That attention to detail is something I want to carry into every musical project I take on.

This website is an evolving record of that journey. As I continue to perform, learn, record, and create, it will become a home for the work I'm proud to share and the projects I'm working toward.

"The goal isn't just to make sound. It's to make someone feel something."

Cruz.Frequency is the creative identity behind this portfolio.
