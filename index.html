04 / About

Artist profile.

I am a percussionist and composer focused on creating music that feels emotional, atmospheric, and meaningful. My work is rooted in performance, but my curiosity extends into orchestral writing, ambient textures, and the art of telling a story through sound.

Studying at The Hartt School has shaped my approach to music deeply. I continue to develop as both a performer and a writer, with a growing interest in cinematic language and the power of a well- placed musical gesture.

“Music is most powerful when it gives a feeling a voice.”

My projects often begin with an image, a mood, or a memory that needs a sound world to match it. Whether the result is a percussion performance, a composition, or a more cinematic atmosphere, I aim for clarity, emotion, and texture in equal measure.

I’m especially interested in the relationship between percussion and narrative—how rhythm, silence, and color can create tension, motion, and release. That interest continues to guide the music I make and the goals I’m building toward.

05 / Commissions

Original music for meaningful moments.

I create custom music for projects that need atmosphere, emotional clarity, and a strong sense of identity. My goal is to write pieces that support the story while leaving a memorable sonic imprint.

Whether the need is a short cue, a character-driven musical moment, or a more immersive ambient texture, I approach each commission with care and attention to the emotional purpose behind the work.
