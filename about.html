04 / About

Artist profile.

I am a percussionist and composer focused on creating music that feels emotional, atmospheric, and meaningful. My work is rooted in performance, but my curiosity extends into orchestral writing, ambient textures, and the art of telling a story through sound.

Studying at The Hartt School has shaped my approach to music deeply. I continue to develop as both a performer and a writer, with a growing interest in cinematic language and the power of a well- placed musical gesture.

“Music is most powerful when it gives a feeling a voice.”

My projects often begin with an image, a mood, or a memory that needs a sound world to match it. Whether the result is a percussion performance, a composition, or a more cinematic atmosphere, I aim for clarity, emotion, and texture in equal measure.

I’m especially interested in the relationship between percussion and narrative—how rhythm, silence, and color can create tension, motion, and release. That interest continues to guide the music I make and the goals I’m building toward.

05 / Biography

Biography.

Just like a piece of fine art, Carter Cruz strives to create music that evokes emotion. As a trained percussionist his professed love for the triangle is undeniable, due to its impactful sound and ability to brighten a musical passage with a subtle touch.

Carter’s musical journey began in middle school and really took off in high school while studying at the Las Vegas Academy of the Arts. There he studied under bands director, John Seaton, who introduced him to the expressive potential of performance and taught him the value of disciplined artistry.

Expanding his passion for music, Carter began dabbling in composition his senior year of high school. He wanted to do something for his final jury that had never been done by a student before, and he embraced that challenge as a way to grow creatively.

A native of Las Vegas, Nevada, Carter currently resides in Connecticut and studies percussion performance at The Hartt School at the University of Hartford. The youngest of three siblings, Carter draws influence from both his family and his surrounding musical communities as he continues to grow as an artist.
