About

Artist profile.

I am a percussionist and composer focused on creating music that feels emotional, atmospheric, and meaningful. My work is rooted in performance, but my curiosity extends into orchestral writing, ambient textures, and the art of telling a story through sound.

Studying at The Hartt School has shaped my approach to music deeply. I continue to develop as both a performer and a writer, with a growing interest in cinematic language and the power of a well- placed musical gesture.

“Music is most powerful when it gives a feeling a voice.”

My projects often begin with an image, a mood, or a memory that needs a sound world to match it. Whether the result is a percussion performance, a composition, or a more cinematic atmosphere, I aim for clarity, emotion, and texture in equal measure.

I’m especially interested in the relationship between percussion and narrative—how rhythm, silence, and color can create tension, motion, and release. That interest continues to guide the music I make and the goals I’m building toward.

Biography

Biography.

Just like a piece of fine art, Carter Cruz strives to create music that evokes emotion. As a trained percussionist his professed love for sound and creativity is undeniable. His playing style is versatile, from hand drums to mallet instruments and smaller accessories, anything he can strike, scrape, tap, rub or rattle is in his wheelhouse. Creating the heartbeat of the song or bringing dynamic color to a musical ensemble through rhythm textures is his primary goal.

Carter’s musical journey began in middle school and really took off in high school while studying at the Las Vegas Academy of the Arts. There he studied under bands director, John Seaton, and instructor, Pat Bowen, who is also the principal percussionist for the Las Vegas Philharmonic. Carter credits his private music teacher, John Freixas, a studio owner and a disciple of the great Louis Bellson—and also toured with Eddie Money, Alice Cooper, Journey, Van Halen and Whitesnake, among others—as the master who helped elevate his musical ability. In 2025, Carter was selected to play in the NMEA All-State High School Band. He was also a musician in the Clark County School District Honor Bands in 2025 & 2026. His junior year of high school, he traveled with the Las Vegas Academy of the Arts Wind Ensemble on a tour that spanned the state of California. The following year, the wind ensemble performed at the Music For All Festival in Indianapolis. Carter has played in many performing arts venues, including The Smith Center, Artemus Ham Hall, McAfee Performing Arts Center, Weill Hall at the Green Music Center, Bob Cole Conservatory of Music Pavilion and Plaza, Beckman YOLA Center, Clowes Memorial Hall, Lincoln Theater and Alfred C. Fuller Music Center.

Expanding his passion for music, Carter began dabbling in composition his senior year of high school. He wanted to do something for his final jury that had never been done by a student before, compose the piece of work he would perform. That’s how “The Beginning of the End” , his inaugural track, was born and a new outlet for creative expression blossomed

A native of Las Vegas, Nevada, Carter currently resides in Connecticut and studies percussion performance at The Hartt School at the University of Hartford. Outside of music, he has shown leadership, resilience and commitment to community service, which helped him attain the rank of Eagle Scout with Scouting America. The youngest of three siblings, he maintains a close relationship with his parents and two sisters.
